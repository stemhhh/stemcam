# Privacy Policy — OBS Phone Cam

Last updated: 2026-11-02

OBS Phone Cam streams camera (and optional microphone) data from your Android phone to OBS Studio on your PC over a local USB connection.

## What we collect

- The app and plugin **do not** upload video, audio, or analytics to our servers.
- Session logs stay on your device until you explicitly use **Share log**.
- No account is required.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — capture for the USB stream
- **Microphone** (optional) — phone audio track in OBS
- **USB accessory** — talk to the OBS plugin
- **Notifications / foreground service** — keep streaming stable

## Third parties

FFmpeg libraries may be bundled with the Windows plugin for decoding. See `THIRD_PARTY_FFMPEG.txt`.

## Contact

Open an issue on the project repository for privacy questions.
